Verifiability as a predictor of automation

If a knowledge profession has immediately and binarily verifiable output, it faces high automation pressure. If success requires qualitative judgement, negotiation, social context, the pressure is lower — at least for now.

High verifiability → high pressureLow verifiability → low pressure
Writing code with testsBusiness strategy
DebuggingNegotiation
Logistics optimisation (routing, scheduling)Leadership
Mathematical proof in a formal systemComplex consultative selling
Structural calculation and physical simulationMedical diagnosis in ambiguous cases
Accounting reconciliationEthical decisions
